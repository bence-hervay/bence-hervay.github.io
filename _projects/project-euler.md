Project Euler is a long collection of problems that are easy to state, hard to
compute, and rewarding exactly to the degree that you refuse to brute-force them.

I have solved around 250 of them, including every one of the first 222 — which puts
me in the top 0.04% of all users. It is the closest thing I have to a hobby that is
also a benchmark.
